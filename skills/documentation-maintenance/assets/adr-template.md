# ADR ID: Short Decision Title

Use the repository's existing decision-record location, filename, and identifier
conventions, including dated slugs where used. Only when no convention exists,
use the next sequential number. Do not rename accepted history to match this
template. Fill in each applicable section, focused on one decision.

## Status

One of: Proposed, Accepted, Superseded by ADR-XXXX, or Deprecated.

State the date the status was set. If superseded, link the replacing ADR.

## Context

Describe the situation and the forces at play. What problem or requirement prompts
a decision? What constraints, assumptions, and non-negotiables apply? Include only
facts relevant to the decision, and link to supporting material rather than
restating it.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two clear sentences, in active voice: "We will ...".
Make it specific enough that a reader can tell whether later work complies.

## Alternatives Considered

List the credible options that were evaluated, and for each, why it was not chosen.
Recording rejected alternatives prevents the same debate from recurring and shows
the decision was deliberate.

## Consequences

Describe what becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include:

* Positive consequences and the benefits gained.
* Negative consequences, trade-offs, and new constraints accepted.
* Follow-up work or migrations the decision requires.

## References

Link related ADRs, issues, specifications, or discussions. Use relative links for
in-repository documents.
